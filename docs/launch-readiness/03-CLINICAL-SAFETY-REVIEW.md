## 4. NEW findings

### CLIN PS-5 — Discontinued or deleted medication keeps reminding · **P1**
**Hazard:** the app instructs a patient by name to take a drug that they or their doctor stopped (H-06, H-09).
**Evidence:** `services/medication.py` performs lifecycle transitions and soft-delete (`:540-630`) and **never touches `MedicationSchedule`** (repo-wide grep: the only importers of `MedicationSchedule` are `services/medication_schedule.py`, `services/patient_dashboard.py:25-30`, `routes/medication_schedule.py:19`, `routes/health_timeline.py:37`, `models/__init__.py`). `deliver_due_reminders` filters on the *schedule* only (`medication_schedule.py:263-275`) and `_med_for_schedule` reloads the drug with no `deleted_at` / `lifecycle_status` filter (`:303-305`), so `med.name` of a deleted drug is still rendered into the reminder body (`:293`). `patient_dashboard.py:54-64` likewise selects due doses with no schedule/medication-state join. The `active`-only gate exists **only at schedule-creation time** (`_load_owned_medication:161-165`).
**Exact fix (two parts, both needed):**
1. `services/medication_schedule.py::deliver_due_reminders` — add `.join(Medication, Medication.id == MedicationSchedule.medication_id)` and `Medication.deleted_at.is_(None), Medication.lifecycle_status == "active"` to the `due` query; mirror the same join in `patient_dashboard.py`'s `due_doses` query and in the `/reminders/due` read-back (`routes/medication_schedule.py:262-273`).
2. `services/medication.py` — on any transition out of `active` (stop / on_hold / entered_in_error / `delete_medication_record`), call `medication_schedule.stop_schedule` for every non-stopped schedule of that medication, inside the same transaction (this reuses `_cancel_open_doses`, so already-materialised doses disappear too).
**Regression tests:** `test_deleted_medication_stops_reminding`, `test_stopped_medication_removed_from_dashboard_actions`.

### CLIN PS-6 — Numeric red flags can never fire (literal-string matching) · **P1**
**Hazard:** severe hypo/hyperglycaemia, hypertensive crisis, and high fever described numerically are **not** escalated; the request goes to the LLM instead of returning the hardcoded "gọi 115" response (H-12).
**Evidence:** `ai/prompt/safety.py:32-34` (`"đường huyết > 400"`, `"glucose > 400"`, `"đường huyết < 50"`, `"glucose < 50"`) and `:48-50` (`"sốt cao > 39"`, `"đường huyết > 300"`, `"huyết áp > 180"`) are **literal strings**, consumed by `phrase in text_lower` at `:251-257`. No patient types the `>` character. Numeric thresholds already exist and are already used elsewhere: `core/clinical_thresholds.py:279-286` → `domain/policies.py:56` → `domain/triage.py:105,142`.
**Exact fix:** add to `SafetyGuard` a numeric extractor run before the phrase scan — regex-capture `(đường huyết|glucose|đh|dh)\s*[:=]?\s*(\d+([.,]\d+)?)\s*(mg/dl|mmol/l)?`, `(huyết áp|ha|bp)\s*[:=]?\s*(\d{2,3})\s*/\s*(\d{2,3})`, `(sốt|nhiệt độ)\s*[:=]?\s*(\d{2}([.,]\d)?)` — normalise mmol/L→mg/dL where a unit is present, default to mg/dL for values >30 and mmol/L otherwise, then compare against `policies.RED_FLAG_VITAL_THRESHOLDS`. Keep the literal list as a fallback.
**Regression tests:** `test_glucose_450_triggers_emergency`, `test_glucose_25_mmol_triggers_emergency`, `test_bp_190_110_triggers_emergency`, `test_normal_glucose_does_not_escalate`.

### CLIN PS-7 — Health timeline surfaces UNVERIFIED lab results as clinical events · **P1**
**Hazard:** an OCR value the patient has *not* confirmed appears as "Xét nghiệm — N chỉ số cần chú ý" with `importance=warning`, and drives the "what got worse" summary (H-10). This directly contradicts the confirmed-only rule enforced for the AI.
**Evidence:** `routes/health_timeline.py:125-132` selects `LabResult` filtered only on `patient_id` + `deleted_at`. Every sibling consumer filters: `routes/lab_intelligence.py:56`, `routes/narrative.py:131`, `routes/patient_insight.py:117`, `domain/longitudinal.py:29`, `ai/context/builder.py:532-533`. Unverified rows demonstrably exist: `services/lab_pipeline.py:183-184` and `services/lab.py:403` store `verified_by_user=not auto_save_blocked`. Consumption points: `domain/health_timeline.py:153-171` (event + importance) and `:429-448` (`summarize` improved/worsened).
**Exact fix:** in `routes/health_timeline.py` add `((LabResult.verified_by_user.is_(True)) | (LabResult.verified_by_doctor.is_(True)))` to the `lab_results` query (and to the `lab_results` argument passed to `engine.summarize` at `:211`). ### CLIN PS-8 — General-report medication re-typing puts a whole OCR line in `Medication.name` · **P1** (escalated from the register's P2 "CLIN PS-3")
**Hazard:** garbage medication names in the record, the medication list, the Meto `medications` context block and the reminder copy (H-02); also the cleanest injection vehicle for AI-F1.
**Evidence:** `extractors_general.py:114-115` re-types any segment matching `_MED_RE` (`:29` — `\d+\s?(mg|mcg|g|ml|viên|vien|iu|ui)\b`) to `medication`, then `:121-122` sets `fields["name"] = content` — the **entire segment**, not a parsed drug name. `MedicationPromoter` validates only non-emptiness (`promoters.py:74-76`) and has no length or shape check. Contrast the prescription path, which splits name / strength / form / frequency (`extractors_prescription.py:76-97`).
**Exact fix:** in `extractors_general.py`, when `_MED_RE` matches, run the segment through `extractors_prescription._parse_medicine(content, None)` and populate `name`/`strength`/`form`/`frequency`/`instructions` from it; if the resulting `name` is empty or longer than a bounded limit (suggest 120 chars), keep the original `ctype` (finding/procedure/…) instead of re-typing. Add a defensive `len(name) <= 120` check in `MedicationPromoter.promote` raising `PromotionInvalid` (→422).
**Regression tests:** `test_general_report_med_line_parses_name_not_whole_sentence`, `test_promoter_rejects_overlong_medication_name`.

### CLIN PS-9 — Meto uses a weaker, parallel safety engine than the rest of the platform · **P2**

## 5. Clinical limitations a pilot patient must be told (consent-form / onboarding copy)

These are **material** and must be stated in Vietnamese, in the app, before first use. Current copy covers items 1, 2 and 7 only (`vi.ts:92-93`, `:177`, `:127-128`).

1. **Meto is not a doctor and does not diagnose.** It explains data you have already confirmed. — *covered* (`vi.ts:92-93`).
2. **Nothing read from a photo is saved automatically.** You must check and confirm every item; what you confirm is what enters your record. — *covered* (`vi.ts:177`).
3. **OCR makes mistakes, including on numbers.** Always compare the drug name, strength and the lab value + unit against the paper document before confirming. The app cannot detect a plausible-but-wrong number.
4. **Handwritten documents are not reliably read.** Only printed prescriptions/reports are supported in this pilot (`extractors_prescription.py:3-7`).
5. **Reminders are in-app only.** There is **no** push notification in this pilot (`notification_transport.py:9-15`, `:76-80`) — if the app is closed you will not be alerted. Do not rely on MetoCare as your only medication reminder.
6. **If you stop a medication, also stop its reminder schedule.** Until CLIN PS-5 is fixed, deleting the drug does not stop its reminders.
7. **Consultation content is informational** and does not replace an in-person examination — *covered* (`vi.ts:127-128`).
8. **In an emergency, call 115 — do not ask Meto.** Meto's emergency detection is keyword-based and will miss symptoms described in numbers, in English, or without Vietnamese diacritics (CLIN PS-6).
9. **Your confirmed health data is sent to a third-party AI provider** to generate answers when Meto is enabled; you can turn each data category off at any time (`vi.ts:265-284`). The provider's retention posture is **UNVERIFIED** — see `07-AI-SAFETY-EVALUATION.md` §2.
10. **This pilot runs on staging with synthetic data**; documents may be lost on redeploy (PROD-F1) and payments are mock.
